Privacy Policy

Updated November 8, 2026

Who we are

WalkInTubsForElderly.com is an information site published by Aging Safely Baths & More, LLC ("Aging Safely Baths," "we," "us"). Call 1-888-779-2284 or email sales@agingsafelybaths.com. The site offers guides for families. When you ask for a quote, the site sends your request to Aging Safely Baths.

What the quote form collects

The form asks for your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code and a short message about what you need. It also records the page you were on. We use this to answer your request, prepare a quote and follow up about it. We do not ask for payment details, Social Security numbers or health records. Please do not type them into the message box.

How your request is sent

Your request goes to our lead server at clients.eyetoad.com. It is operated for Aging Safely Baths by Eye To Ad Media. The server stores the request and emails our sales team. A copy may also go through FormSubmit, the form processor this site already uses. FormSubmit has its own privacy policy, which governs how it handles that copy. These providers handle your information only to deliver it to us.

Calls and texts

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request. Agreeing to calls or texts is not a condition of buying anything. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to end texts. You can also tell us on a call to stop contacting you.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are useful. It sets cookies and collects data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location. Analytics data is kept for 14 months. Read Google's privacy policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the providers named above, with installers or partners who help fill your request, when the law requires it, or with your permission.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests for as long as we need them to serve you and run our business, then delete or anonymize them. If you ask us to delete your information sooner, we will do that unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email sales@agingsafelybaths.com or call 1-888-779-2284. You can also stop calls and texts at any time, as described above.

Children

This site is for adults, including family caregivers. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, so what you send is encrypted in transit. We collect no payment data here. No method of sending data over the internet is perfectly secure, but we limit access to your request to the people and systems that need it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
